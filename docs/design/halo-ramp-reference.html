About

How it speaks

Clock, Alarm and Timer can each say the time aloud at the interval you choose, even with your iPhone locked.

An alarm plays its tune and says its time until you stop or snooze it.

Use your iPhone's alarm volume, and test an alarm with your iPhone locked before relying on it.

Private by design

Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no tracking.

    Blending

    • Blended in OKLCH (keeps saturation, never greys) with a smoothstep ease inside each ramp
    • Dark-to-pale crossings take 20 min and pass through muted plum, rose and coral (twilight chroma ×0.50)
    • Displayed values ease toward the target with a 0.5 s time constant
    • Text is dark or light, never grey: it switches near sky luminance 0.21 (0.204 / 0.222 with hysteresis), in one step together with the glass
    • Behind light text, glass gains a navy scrim that keeps panels dark enough for small text
    • Secondary text rises from 58% opacity as needed to keep 4.5:1 on sky and glass
    • Dark uses the same stops, near black with lights at 12–20%

    Type

    • Newsreader, optical size 72, weight 280 for times (clock and ringing 112 pt, timer 92, list 38)
    • Newsreader italic 350 for sentences, labels and am/pm
    • Geist 400–600, 11–17 pt for controls

    Behaviour

    • Clock ring fills to the next spoken time; timer ring empties as time runs out
    • Speaking: an accent glow breathes behind the ring (1.6 s); ringing pulses faster (0.9 s)
    • The sky drifts slowly, over 34 s
    • Selected options use the text colour; accent is kept for progress, toggles and the main action
