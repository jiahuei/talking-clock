# Talking Clock - Support

## How to Use

1. The app displays the current time with digital and optional analog clock
2. Set your announcement interval using the dropdown menus
3. Enable "Auto-Announce Time" to hear automatic announcements
4. Tap "Speak Time" button to hear time immediately
5. Toggle "Show Analog Clock" to show/hide the analog clock face

## Features

### Digital Clock
- Large, easy-to-read time display in HH:mm:ss format
- Current date displayed below the time
- Updates every second
- Supports both light and dark mode

### Analog Clock
- Optional canvas-drawn clock face
- Hour, minute, and second hands
- Hour markers at 12, 3, 6, and 9 o'clock positions
- Toggle visibility with the "Show Analog Clock" switch

### Time Announcements
- **Auto-Announce**: Automatically speaks the time at your chosen interval
- **Manual Announce**: Tap "Speak Time" button anytime
- **Background Support**: Continues announcing even when app is in background
- Uses high-quality iOS text-to-speech engine

### Intervals Available
**Seconds**: 10, 20, 30
**Minutes**: 1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 30

### Settings Persistence
All your preferences are automatically saved:
- Selected interval value and unit
- Analog clock visibility preference
- Auto-announce enabled/disabled state

## Troubleshooting

### No voice announcements
- Ensure device volume is up
- Check that device is not in silent mode (flip the physical switch on the side)
- Verify that "Auto-Announce Time" toggle is enabled
- Grant notification permission when prompted

### Announcements stop in background
- Ensure notifications are allowed:
  - Go to iOS Settings > Talking Clock > Notifications
  - Enable "Allow Notifications"
- Check that Low Power Mode is not active:
  - Settings > Battery > Low Power Mode should be OFF
- Verify Background App Refresh is enabled:
  - Settings > General > Background App Refresh

### Manual "Speak Time" button not working
- Check device volume
- Ensure device is not muted
- Try restarting the app
- Check if other apps can play audio

### Settings not saving
- Close and reopen the app completely (swipe up from app switcher)
- Restart your device
- Ensure you have sufficient storage space

### App crashes or freezes
- Force quit the app and reopen
- Restart your iPhone/iPad
- Check for iOS updates in Settings > General > Software Update
- Reinstall the app if problem persists

### Clock time is incorrect
- The app uses your device's system time
- Check Settings > General > Date & Time
- Enable "Set Automatically" for accurate time

## Permissions

### Notifications
Required for background time announcements. When you put the app in the background, notifications allow the app to alert you at your chosen interval.

**To enable**: Tap "Allow" when prompted on first launch, or go to Settings > Talking Clock > Notifications.

### Audio
Required for playing voice announcements. The app uses iOS text-to-speech to speak the time.

**To enable**: Automatically enabled when you allow the app to use audio.
